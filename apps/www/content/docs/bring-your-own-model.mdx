Amical supports local Whisper models and hosted speech models configured through the AI Models settings.

## Speech Models

Open:

`Settings -> AI Models -> Speech`

From there you can choose:

- Local Whisper models downloaded to your machine
- Amical Cloud
- Groq Whisper Large v3 Turbo
- Groq Whisper Large v3
- Aqua Avalon 1.5

Hosted providers require their provider API key before they can be selected.

## Groq Long-Form Dictation

Groq speech models are the expected hosted provider for long hands-free dictation. Amical keeps push-to-talk responsive with short low-latency chunks, while hands-free dictation uses longer Groq chunks and a guarded final pass to reduce pause-induced loops.
